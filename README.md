# Course Grade Calculator

A static, student-friendly calculator for tracking course grades, remaining course points, passing requirements, and the final exam score needed to reach 70%.

## Using the calculator
To use the calculator just insert all the grades receives so far. The current grade given is based on the points obtained so far in the course over the total ponits given so far. 

For example, the final exam is worth 16% of the grade, therefore if you do all the works/exams/assigments except for the final that means that at most you can accumulate 84 point of the final grade. If out of that you've obtained 56.59, then you current grade would be: 56.59/84 = 67.4%

The calculator also provides a button that tells you how much you need in the final to reach a passing grade in the course (reach a 70).

Important:
- Bonus points only count towards the section they were earned (unless told otherwise). For example, bonus earned for the labs is exclusively for the lab grade.
- Include the bonus points when entering the grades, the page handles spreadding the points. If you exceed the amount of points you can reach in a section, it caps at the maximum weight. For example, exams are worth 40% (40 points) therefore you cannot get more than 40 from exams. 

### Terms in the calculator
- Current grade: This is your the grade at the moment. It's calculated as: (points earned)/(points offered so far)
- Course Points Earned: Points you have accumulated from your work in the course.
- Points offered so far: The maximum amount of points you could have accumulated so far. 
- Requirements check: To pass the course you must have an avergae grade of 50% in the labs and 50% in each Programming Assingment (PA).
